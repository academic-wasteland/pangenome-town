From a phenotype profile to candidate genes.

Try a Marfan syndrome example: ectopia lentis, arachnodactyly and aortic root aneurysm. The expected disease-associated gene is FBN1. Discover Ubar’s service, run INDIGENA, and inspect the gene candidates and their provenance.

The trained model ranks mouse phenotype profiles. Human orthologues are annotated from MGI; scores are not disease probabilities or a validated human diagnosis.

The phenotype profile

Searches HPO and MP labels and exact synonyms. Use HPO: or MP: when a label exists in both; identifiers also work. Up to 30 terms; no patient identifiers.

Why FBN1? Read the disease reference. This is a selected demonstration case, not an independent accuracy benchmark.

Yamatai keeps the VCF, genotype and sample ID. Ubar first receives phenotypes only, then just the selected allele and phenotypes. Public demo data are synthetic.

Edit the phenotype labels and click Run my phenotype query to execute INDIGENA live, without audio. You can change the terms and run again when it finishes. Narration is optional.
